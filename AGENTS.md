# rekorn.com

Public professional portfolio of Taejoon Park (Rekorn).

- `_data/profile.json` contains public professional facts. Keep role dates and release
  status consistent. Do not invent revenue, team staffing, metrics or testimonials.
- `tools/build_portfolio.py` renders the Korean and English homepages and sitemap.
  Run it after editing the data or template. `index.html` and `en/index.html` are outputs.
- Preserve `/legacy/`, its posts and the old URL redirects. The original DJ site is
  retained at branch `archive/dj-before-portfolio-20260923` (commit 77e6678).
- GitHub Pages deploys `master` at repository root. Keep `CNAME` as `rekorn.com`.
- Use real book/game/profile/album assets. Check the official release and existing
  archive before drawing substitutes. Do not publish placeholder artwork when the
  real asset is available. Preserve artwork and its credited artist.
  Keep copy specific to actual work and the reader.
  Do not add fake reviews, made-up counters, autoplay media or unnecessary animation.
- Never copy credentials, qualification IDs, private contacts, client material or
  internal business records into this public repository.
- Verify desktop/mobile layouts, English/Korean, navigation, images and archive routes
  after changes. Respect reduced motion and keyboard focus. Confirm the deployed result.
- Preserve legacy theme attribution. Avoid unrelated source, dependency or account changes.
